Team

Remonda Hassan — Founder

My name is Remonda, and I created Figeographie to showcase the extraordinary world that we live in. The name “Figeographie” was inspired by the first deliberate photo I ever took — a fig on the ground during a walk to school with my sister. Each day, we would pass a beautiful fig tree bursting with ripe fruit. Many would fall, and we’d look for the uncracked ones to snack on as we walked. My appreciation for our world blossomed from that simple joy. That fig, and that moment, captured the joy and sweetness I find in nature. It became the symbol of my love for photography and my desire to make this simple peace and joy ubiquitous globally.

Hend Hassan — Charity Director

I joined Figeographie because I believe that spreading good only results in receiving good. Figeographie’s motto of spreading the beauty of life, whether it’s by simply appreciating a little flower or helping a little child in need, appeals to my heart, and is what inspired me to lead Figeographie’s charities to do my duty of helping humanity.

Seneca Wattanapanich

My name is Seneca, and I joined Figeographie because I wanted to share my love for nature photography. My passion stemmed from my dad. I began taking photos when I was just 11 years old. He introduced this art to me and soon enough it became more than just a hobby! It's a way for me to preserve and continue our little tradition that means a lot to me. Now it's a meaningful part of family trips and I can document the world as we explore!

Cate Wells

Being surrounded by beauty, whether it’s in a painting or a mountain view, reminds me of how precious our world is. That’s why I believe saving the Earth is our responsibility. It is our shared home, and we all have a part in protecting it.
