Within Egypt, cardiovascular disease is the leading cause of death out of all non-communicable diseases, responsible for 275,665 deaths in 2021 alone. Risk factors associated with cardiovascular disease continue to rise at an alarming rate. Rheumatic heart diseases are still responsible for approximately 2,000 deaths per year and the death of 28% Egyptian babies with congenital heart disease.
The Magdi Yacoub Heart Foundation (MYF) is an Egyptian registered charity founded in 2008 by Sir Magdi Yacoub, Nobel Prize winner Dr. Ahmed Zewail, and Ambassador Mohamed Shaker. Fully funded by donations, the foundation provides free, world-class cardiovascular care to patients in Egypt.
Figeographie is proud to support the Magdi Yacoub Heart Foundation in their mission to provide life-saving cardiac care to those in need. We are committed to raising $10,000 to fund the full cost of an open-heart surgical operation at Aswan Heart Centre, where over 1,000 surgical operations are performed annually.
